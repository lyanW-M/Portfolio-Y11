The Lore of DOOM

The DOOM video games series consist of first-person shooters in which the player controls an unnamed space marine commonly referred to as Doomguy. In the 2016 series, the protagonist is called the "Doom Slayer" or just "Slayer" in later entries. The player battles the forces of Hell, consisting of demons and the undead in order to save planet Earth and stop the extinction of the human race.

(Do note DOOM wasn't made as a story game in it's early days so some info may or may not be confusing to the reader)

Doom (1993)

Doomguy gets sent to Mars after he punches and disobeys his superior officer who tasked him to shoot civilians for an unknown reason, as you head to the Mars Research Ship that is researching teleportation technology, you realize demons are coming out of these portals and has taken over the space ship and Mars itself.

After Doomguy first goes to one of Mars's moons, Phobos, and kills every demon there, he later goes to the next moon using the teleporter on Phobos to Deimos to hell itself. He fights his way through hordes of demons and eventually kills the mastermind behind this invasion, a cybernetic spider demon (Bro's a literal one man army). He escapes back to Earth using a teleporter only to realize Earth has also been invaded by demons, 60% of the human population has been killed and devoured but most importantly, his beloved pet rabbit, Daisy, has been killed

Doom I I - (Hell on Earth)

The player once again takes the role of a lone Marine, Doomguy, who, after escaping Hell and being the only survivor of the Mars mission, returns to Earth. He discovers that Earth has been invaded by Hell's demon army. With all the major cities of the world in ruins, the remaining leaders plan to use spacecraft to evacuate the survivors of Earth's population. However, the starport is the only way for the ships to depart and the demons have protected it with a force field. All of humanity's remaining soldiers make a desperate assault on the starport, but eventually they are decimated and only Doomguy remains. The Marine manages to enter the infested starport, slay all the demons in his way and is able to shut down the force field. The surviving humans escape, and he sits quietly waiting for death, knowing he saved humanity from extinction.

Suddenly, the remaining humans discover the source of the hellish invasion: the Marine's hometown. He gets back into the fight and exterminates the hellspawn from the town, and finds another entryway into Hell. To close the portal, he must enter Hell again to stop the invasion. After journeying through its twisted surface, the Marine manages to confront the Icon of Sin, a gigantic demon, and with sheer will, kills it (Like I said, literal one man army). Its gruesome death causes devastation on Hell, and the portal to Earth has been sealed.

Final DOOM (TNT Evilution)

The UAC is once again experimenting with dimensional gateway technology. They set up a research base in Jupiter where they start experimenting and now there were marines to protect these experiments. The first experiment was a success as when the demons came out of the portal, the marines successfully defeated them before they could start a big invasion. As time goes on, the UAC and the marines start becoming more careless and unaware from the countless successful experiments until one day, a big ship from a portal to hell comes into the UAC's radar and the security assumes it was an energy transport, but when the ship gets closer, the people in the base realize from the grotesque shape it has with flesh, bones, and corruption, that it was a ship containing demons, and before they could do anything, the invasion has begun and has killed everyone onboard Jupiter. Luckily Doomguy was having a nice walk during this invasion and was only attacked by a demon imp before quickly finishing it off with a shotgun. Doomguy realizes there is an invasion and vows to avenge his fallen comrades and kill every demon

In the end after defeating countless demons yet again, the marine encounters and defeats the Icon of Sin yet again and the game describes "something rumbles in the distance. A blue light glows within the ruined skull of the demon-spitter." (Icon of Sin is also known as the demon-spitter)

Final DOOM (The Plutonia Experiment)

UAC's scientists start working on quantum accelerator devices intended to close interdimensional gates and stop possible invasions. The experiments are carried out in a secret research complex, with a stationed detachment of marines. The work seems to be going well, but... The creatures from Outside have their dire attention drawn towards the new research. A gate opens in the heart of the complex and unnatural horrors pour out. The quantum accelerator performs superbly — the gate is quickly closed and the invasion stopped. Research continues more boldly. On the next day, a ring of seven gates opens and an even greater invasion commences. For a single hour the quantum accelerators manage to close six of the seven gates... but the hellish army has become too numerous and too strong. The complex is overrun. Everyone present is slain, or zombified. The last gate of Hell remains open, manned and guarded by a demon Gatekeeper.

The government, frantic that the quantum accelerator will be destroyed or used in some evil alien fashion against humanity, orders all marines to the site at once. Doomguy is the closest one to the site and gets there first. There he concludes that by the time reinforcements arrive that the demons will be too numerous and powerful. The marine decides to enter the complex and stop the Gatekeeper alone.

Doom 64

A mysterious entity known as the Mother Demon has not only been making more new demons but also ressurecting the rotting corpses of the dead marines in the now abandoned UAC ships of Mars. Doomguy, now suffering from severe PTSD and "combat fatigue" after the first two games, is sent back to the planetary moons as the only person with the experience to handle such a threat. As Doomguy slaughters demons to get to the Mother Demon, he realizes that as long as he lives, hell will keep trying to get him due to him being the only living being to ever stop multiple massive hell invasions and was deemed an anomaly and an embarassment to hell that has to be crushed. As Doomguy goes through a portal travels to the heart of hell, he meets and kills the Mother Demon and he decides that instead of going back to Earth, he decides to stay in hell and keep killing demons due to the fact that he will just keep bringing more demons to Earth. Now, he stays in hell and fights demons for eternity

Doom 64 (The Lost Levels)

Following the defeat of the Mother of All Demons (Mother Demon), Doomguy spents time tearing up Hell. The Mother Demon's sister Resurrector banishes the Marine to a distant vacated base on the surface of Sedna and tries to kill him. He fought his way through the base to find a portal back to Hell. He fights his way through this new section of Hell, and takes revenge on the sister and once again decides to fight in hell for eternity.
